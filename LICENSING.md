# WealthLens licensing map

Last updated: 2026-08-12

WealthLens uses different standard licences for different kinds of material:

- dashboard frontend, API, and data pipelines: GPL-3.0-only;
- simulator core under packages/wealthlens-sim/: AGPL-3.0-or-later;
- documentation, reports, methods notes, and registries: CC-BY-4.0 unless a
  more specific notice applies; and
- synthetic public datasets: CC0-1.0 unless a more specific source or output
  notice applies.

The root [LICENSE](LICENSE) contains the complete GPLv3 terms for the software
boundary. The simulator's licence is in
[packages/wealthlens-sim/LICENSE](packages/wealthlens-sim/LICENSE). Source
datasets, charts, and third-party materials retain their recorded licences and
attribution requirements; consult the data-licence registries before reuse.

The software boundary changed from MIT to GPL-3.0-only on 12 August 2026. See
[RELICENSING.md](RELICENSING.md) and [LICENSES/MIT.txt](LICENSES/MIT.txt) for
the transition record and retained notice.
