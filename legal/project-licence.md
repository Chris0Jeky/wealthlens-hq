# Project licence notes

The repository's current multi-licence boundaries are authoritative in
[LICENSING.md](../LICENSING.md). The former MIT software boundary moved to
GPL-3.0-only by owner decision on 12 August 2026.

## Current status

- Dashboard frontend, API, and data pipelines: GPL-3.0-only.
- Simulator core: AGPL-3.0-or-later.
- Documentation, reports, methods notes, and registries: CC-BY-4.0 unless a
  more specific notice applies.
- Synthetic public datasets: CC0-1.0 unless a more specific notice applies.

Earlier versions and copies released under MIT keep their existing grants;
see [RELICENSING.md](../RELICENSING.md) and
[LICENSES/MIT.txt](../LICENSES/MIT.txt). Source data and third-party materials
retain their recorded licences and attribution requirements.
